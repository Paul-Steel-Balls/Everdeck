EVERDECK PATCH NOTES

==================================================
v0.9.3: The first big balance pass
==================================================

Heads up: your favorite Boss might feel different after this one.
That's on purpose. This is the first of several balance passes, and
there's more tuning to come.

How we balance: the computer plays itself thousands of times with the
strongest decks it can find for every Boss. We change as little as we
can, test again, and only ship what makes the game more even. Before
this patch, the best Boss won about 61% of games and the worst won
about 37%. After it, every Boss wins between about 47% and 56%.

"LUCKY" LARRY
  - Feeling Lucky (Signature Move): now deals 2 damage to a random
    enemy (was 1).
  - Dice Boy: now 2/2 (was 1/2).
  - Chip: now 2/2 (was 1/2).
  - Lucky Seven: now 5/5 (was 5/4).
  - The Whale: now 5/5 (was 5/4).
  Larry was the weakest Boss by a long way. Now he isn't.

CAPTAIN BARNACLE
  - Hire a Deckhand (Signature Move): the Deckhand is now 1/2 (was 1/1).
  Barnacle has no card draw, so he needs his crew to stick around.

GRANDMA AGATHA
  - A Nice Cup of Tea (Signature Move): now restores 2 Health (was 3).
  Agatha was one of the two strongest Bosses, and her healing beat
  Presto far too often.

Still on our list for next time:
  - Grunk is still a little strong.
  - Agatha still beats Presto more often than she should.
  - Echo Blast is being watched.

You need v0.9.3 to play online. Older versions can't connect.

==================================================
v0.9.2: Payoffs are capped
==================================================

  - Firecracker Mage, Final Exam, Thesis Defense and Graduation Day now
    count at most 3 other cards played this turn. Presto could deal 40
    damage in one turn before; he can no longer kill from full Health.
  - The computer opponent now uses the Lucky Penny properly.
